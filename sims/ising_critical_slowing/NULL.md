# Null model — ising_critical_slowing

Co-authored (2026-11-01). CC0. Written before run.py existed.

`time_shuffled_series`: the same |m| series with its time order shuffled. Its integrated
autocorrelation time is the estimator's floor (about 0.5 sweeps, by the convention used). A tau
near that floor means no memory is measured; critical slowing has to rise well above it.
